What the verdict means

SAFE: No conflict found in the visible, readable label. This is not a guarantee.

WARNING: The label is incomplete, an additive source is uncertain, or cross-contact is mentioned.

AVOID: A visible ingredient conflicts with a selected restriction.

Important safety note

Ingredient origins may vary by manufacturer. INS codes alone do not always prove the source. For severe allergies, contact the manufacturer and follow medical advice. Jain practices can vary; Dietrich applies conservative screening rules.
